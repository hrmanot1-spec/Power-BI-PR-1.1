2:45–4:15 — Show Page 1. Explain Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity and Average Discount cards; then Sales by Category and Profit by Region bar charts.

Before exporting: check face and screen are both visible, voice is clear, duration is 5–10 minutes, file is MP4, then upload to Google Drive or YouTube (unlisted) and paste the real share link into README.
